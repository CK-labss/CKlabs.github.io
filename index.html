Our Story

Built by Students,
For Everyone

CK Labs was founded on the conviction that great science education shouldn't be gated behind tuition fees or geography. Anyone should be able to easily be able to enter STEM regardless of income, gender, beliefs, and sexuality. We seek to ensure everyone gets a place in STEM.

Every course we design begins with a simple question: what does it actually feel like to think like a scientist? That means real equipment, real data, and instructors who understand what makes a course interesting.

We partner with local schools, libraries, and community centers to extend our reach.
