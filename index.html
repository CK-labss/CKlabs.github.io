Our Story

Built by Educators,
For Everyone

CK Labs was founded on the conviction that great science education shouldn't be gated behind tuition fees or geography. We started as a weekend workshop in a shared community space and have grown into a full curriculum provider trusted by schools, families, and adult learners across the region.

Every course we design begins with a simple question: what does it actually feel like to think like a scientist? That means real equipment, real data, and instructors who are working professionals in their fields — not just teachers reading from a textbook.

We partner with local schools, libraries, and community centers to extend our reach, and we offer sliding-scale pricing and full scholarships to ensure no motivated learner is turned away.
